## 2. Shipping & Delivery Policy (`/shipping-policy`)

### Delivery Charges Table

| Zone | Flowers / Perishables | Books / Hampers | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jabalpur ≤5 km | Free >₹1,500 / ₹49 below | Same | Same-day (before 1 PM) |
| Jabalpur 5–15 km | Free >₹1,500 / ₹99 below | ₹79 | Same/Next-day |
| Jabalpur 15–30 km | ₹149 flat | ₹99 flat | Next-day |
| Pan-India | Not available | Calculated at checkout | 3–7 business days |

### Order Status Lifecycle
`Processing` → `Shipped` → `Out for Delivery` → `Delivered` → `Cancelled`

### 8 Detailed Delivery Rules
1. Same-day cutoff at 1:00 PM IST
2. Morning (9–1) & Evening (3–7) delivery slots
3. OTP-based delivery confirmation
4. Failed delivery re-attempt: +₹30–₹50 charge
5. Outstation dispatched within 1–2 business days
6. Incorrect address: re-delivery charges apply
7. Peak season delays: 1–2 extra days (Valentine's, Diwali, etc.)
8. Outstation floral orders not available

## 3. Refund & Return Policy (`/refund-policy`)

### Eligibility Summary

| Category | Returnable | Refundable | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh Flowers | ❌ | Conditional | 2 hrs of delivery |
| Cafe Goods | ❌ | Conditional | 2 hrs of delivery |
| Books | ✅ | Full | 7 days |
| Gift Hampers (non-perishable) | ✅ | Partial | 7 days |
| Custom / Personalized Orders | ❌ | No | N/A |

### 5-Step Refund Process
1. Document the issue (photos)
2. Contact within the eligibility window
3. Verification & approval (within 24 hrs)
4. Return pickup/drop-off if required
5. Refund in **3–5 business days** to original payment method

### Non-Refundable Situations
- Flowers damaged by recipient after delivery
- Requests outside the window
- Custom orders after production starts
- Change of mind (perishables)

## 4. Customization Guide (`/customization`)

### Four Customizable Categories

| Type | Lead Time | Extra Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Custom Bouquets | 24 hours | No charge for colour changes |
| Custom Gift Hampers | 48 hours | +₹99–₹199 for premium box/label |
| Custom Cafe Orders | 48–72 hours | Quoted individually |
| Curated Book Packs | 24–48 hours | Free |

### Customization Options
- **Bouquets:** colour palette, size (S/M/L/Grand), wrap style, add-ons, message card
- **Hampers:** item mix, box style, tissue/filler, custom label, message card
- **Cafe:** flavour, dietary needs (eggless/vegan/GF/sugar-free), custom text, bulk
- **Books:** genre, level, theme, personality brief, bookmark add-ons

### Process (5 Steps)
1. Tell us your vision (checkout notes or Contact page)
2. Team consults via call/WhatsApp within 4 hrs
3. 50% advance payment for orders >₹1,000
4. Progress photo shared for orders >₹2,000
5. Delivery or store pickup at Ranjhi

### Key Rules
- **Non-refundable** once production starts
- 50% advance is non-refundable
- Corporate/bulk: email corporate@rainashaven.com
